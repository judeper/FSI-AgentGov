## Lens for this area (plus the shared rubric)
- **Task orientation** — playbooks are step-by-step procedures; can an admin actually
  *follow them to done*? Missing prerequisites, decision points, verification, rollback?
- **The 4-playbooks-per-control pattern** — is it clear, consistent, and the right
  granularity? Is navigating control → its playbooks obvious?
- **Scannability** — numbered steps, screenshots/placeholders, copy-paste blocks, est. time.
- **Coverage & organisation** (use the inventory) — gaps, duplication, naming, grouping
  (control-implementations vs governance-operations vs compliance-and-audit vs
  incident-and-risk vs agent-lifecycle).
- **Findability** — when an admin needs "how do I do X", can they get to the right playbook?

# Brief 1 — Controls

{{PROVENANCE}}

## Your task
Review **only the Controls area** for user experience and propose improvements.

## Where it is in the package
- `area-1-controls.md` — the 10-section control template, the master index, and all
  **79 controls** in full (4 pillars: Security, Management, Reporting, SharePoint).
- For look-and-feel: open the matching pages in `_rendered-site/controls/…` (or `_pdf/`).
- Context: `00-START-HERE.md`, `00-SITE-MAP.md`.

## Lens for this area (plus the shared rubric)
- Is the **10-section template** the right shape for a busy M365 admin? Too long, too
  short, wrong order? Is the BLUF/summary doing its job?
- **Scannability & consistency** across 79 controls — headings, tables (zone matrix,
  roles, related controls), terminology (canonical role names), verification steps.
- **Task orientation** — from landing on a control, can the admin tell *what to do*,
  *who does it*, and *how to verify*? Where do they get stuck?
- **Findability** — index/grouping/pillar structure; is the Control Explorer (if shown)
  helping? How would an admin find "the control for X"?
- **Density vs. usefulness** — does the volume help or overwhelm?

Use `briefs/REVIEWER-RUBRIC.md`; return findings in `briefs/RESPONSE-TEMPLATE.md`
(ID prefix `CONTROLS-`). Cite specific controls/sections. Critique what exists; propose
concrete, prioritised changes. No repository access needed.

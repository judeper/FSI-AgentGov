## Your task
Review **only the Assessment experience** (the interactive readiness tool) and propose
improvements.

## Where it is in the package
- `area-3-assessment.md` — the landing page, the SPA's user-facing strings
  (`i18n/en.json`: labels/questions/results copy), a walkthrough, and an engine summary.
- **Run it:** open `_rendered-site/assessment/index.html` (client-side, no server) — this
  is the real interactive UX. PDFs in `_pdf/` show the static view.
- Context: `00-START-HERE.md`.

## Lens for this area (plus the shared rubric)
- **Flow & friction** — from arrival → answering → scoring → results → export. Where would
  a US-FSI admin hesitate, abandon, or mis-answer? Is progress/length clear?
- **Input design** (the "user input" lens) — question wording, answer options, defaults,
  validation, autosave/resume, jargon, cognitive load.
- **Output & value** — are results/recommendations clear, credible, and *actionable*?
  Is the export (PDF/JSON/XLSX) useful? Does the tool feel worth completing?
- **Trust & expectation-setting** — does the user understand what it does, how long it
  takes, what they get, and how data is handled (it's client-side)?
- **Discoverability** — is the assessment surfaced as a primary entry point, or buried?

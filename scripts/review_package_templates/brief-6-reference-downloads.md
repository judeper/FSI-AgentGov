# Brief 6 — Reference & Downloads

{{PROVENANCE}}

## Your task
Review **only the Reference materials and Downloads** for user experience and propose
improvements.

## Where it is in the package
- `area-6-reference-and-downloads.md` — glossary, RACI / role catalog, regulatory
  mappings, license requirements, CSA materials, and the Downloads section (role-based
  Excel checklists).
- Look-and-feel: `_rendered-site/reference/…` and `_rendered-site/downloads/…`.
- Context: `00-START-HERE.md`, `00-SITE-MAP.md`.

## Lens for this area (plus the shared rubric)
- **Fitness for purpose** — reference pages are look-up tools. Are they scannable,
  well-tabulated, and quick to use? Or wall-of-text?
- **Findability & linking** — are reference items linked from where they're needed
  (e.g., role names → role catalog; regulations → mappings)? Could they be?
- **Glossary / terminology** — coverage, clarity, consistency with the rest of the site.
- **Regulatory mappings** — is the control↔regulation crosswalk usable for a compliance
  reader, and appropriately hedged (no overclaims)?
- **Downloads** — are the Excel checklists discoverable, clearly described, and obviously
  valuable? Is it clear which role downloads what and why?

Use `briefs/REVIEWER-RUBRIC.md`; return findings in `briefs/RESPONSE-TEMPLATE.md`

**Alert Tuning Guide (docs/alert-tuning-guide.md):**

Structure:
1. **Header:** "Alert Tuning Guide", part of Agent Observability Foundation
2. **Overview:** How to calibrate dynamic thresholds for zone-appropriate alerting
3. **Baseline Period:**
   - Minimum: 3 days (30+ data points at hourly granularity)
   - Standard: 10 days (captures daily patterns)
   - Full: 3 weeks (captures weekly patterns)
   - During baseline: Alerts in "Learning" state, no notifications
   - Document the ~14 days recommendation per user context
4. **Sensitivity Levels:**
   - Low: Fewer alerts, higher deviation tolerance (Zone 1 default)
   - Medium: Balanced detection (Zone 2 default)
   - High: More alerts, lower tolerance (Zone 3 default)
   - Include table: Sensitivity -> Approximate Deviation Tolerance -> Recommended Zone
5. **Tuning Process:**
   - Week 1-2: Observe alert volume per zone
   - Week 3: Adjust if Zone 1 too noisy (Low -> custom threshold), Zone 3 too quiet (verify data quality)
   - Month 2: Fine-tune failingPeriods if needed
   - Include ARM parameter locations where sensitivity is configured
6. **Common Issues:**
   - No alerts firing: Check data volume, verify baseline period elapsed
   - Too many alerts: Reduce sensitivity or increase failingPeriods.minFailingPeriodsToAlert
   - Alerts only during business hours: Expected for usage-based alerts, consider different thresholds
7. **Zone-Specific Recommendations:**
   - Zone 1 (Personal): Expect higher variance, Low sensitivity appropriate
   - Zone 2 (Team): Moderate variance, Medium sensitivity baseline
   - Zone 3 (Enterprise): Low variance expected, High sensitivity justified by risk
8. **Ignoring Historical Anomalies:** Document `ignoreDataBefore` property for excluding known anomalous periods

Use hedging language per CONTRIBUTING.md.

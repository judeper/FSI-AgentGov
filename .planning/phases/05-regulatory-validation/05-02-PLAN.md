  <files>.planning/phases/05-regulatory-validation/FINRA-2026-REPORT-ANALYSIS.md</files>
  <action>
Extract AI/agent-relevant findings from the FINRA 2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report and map each finding to specific framework controls.

**Step 1: Access the FINRA 2026 Report**
- Use WebFetch to access: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2026-annual-regulatory-oversight-report
- Also try the PDF: https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/2026-annual-regulatory-oversight-report.pdf
- If direct access fails, use WebSearch: "FINRA 2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report GenAI AI supervision"
- Supplement with secondary analysis sources from research (Debevoise, Snell & Wilmer)

**Step 2: Extract AI/agent-relevant findings ONLY**
Look for content related to:
- GenAI use cases and supervision expectations
- AI agent autonomy classification and supervision
- Audit trail completeness (prompts, model state, reasoning retention)
- Decision reconstruction requirements
- AI-generated communications supervision (Rule 2210 intersection)
- Suitability obligations for AI-assisted recommendations (Rule 2111)
- Written Supervisory Procedures (WSP) for AI tools
- Any mention of AI/ML model risk in examination priorities

Ignore:
- Traditional trading topics (market structure, AML, etc.)
- General compliance reminders not specific to AI/agents
- Cybersecurity topics already covered in framework

**Step 3: Map each finding to its underlying FINRA Rule**
Critical: FINRA Report findings are interpretive guidance for existing rules, NOT new rules. Each finding MUST be mapped to the underlying rule:
- Rule 3110 (Supervision) — WSP requirements for AI
- Rule 4511 (Books and Records) — audit trail completeness
- Rule 2111 (Suitability) — AI-assisted recommendations
- Rule 2210 (Communications) — AI-generated content standards
- Rule 3120 (Supervisory Control System) — testing supervisory procedures

**Step 4: Map findings to framework controls**
For each AI-relevant finding, identify:
- Primary control(s) where the finding should be integrated
- Integration approach (add to existing regulatory section, enhance existing guidance)
- Specific text to add (draft integration language using approved regulatory phrases)

Validate the Retention Period Matrix in regulatory-mappings.md against official SEC 17a-4 and CFTC Rule 1.31 text.

**Step 1: Audit current Retention Period Matrix**
Read the existing matrix in regulatory-mappings.md (lines 14-19). Current entries:
- Communications (agent logs, chat, email) → 3 years → SEC 17a-4(b)(4)
- Accounting/Financial Records → 6 years → SEC 17a-4(a)
- Customer Account Records → 6 years after account close → SEC 17a-4(c)(e)(5)
- FINRA-Specific Records → 6 years → FINRA 4511(b)

**Step 2: Verify against SEC 17a-4 full text**
Use WebSearch/WebFetch to access: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/240.17a-4
Verify each subsection:
- 17a-4(a): What specific records require 6-year retention? Verify "accounting/financial records" is accurate characterization
- 17a-4(b): What are ALL subsection categories? Currently only (b)(4) is cited
  - (b)(1): Blotters — verify applicability to AI agents
  - (b)(2): Ledgers — verify applicability
  - (b)(3): Stock certificates — likely N/A
  - (b)(4): Communications — verify 3-year characterization
  - (b)(5)-(b)(17): Check each for AI agent applicability
- 17a-4(c): Account records — verify "6 years after account close"
- 17a-4(e)(5): Verify this subsection exists and describes what current matrix claims
- 17a-4(f): WORM/audit-trail requirements — verify October 2022 amendment (effective May 2023)

**Step 3: Verify CFTC Rule 1.31 retention periods**
Use WebSearch to access: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/1.31
- Verify 5-year minimum retention period
- Verify "first 2 years readily accessible" requirement
- Verify "life of enterprise plus 5 years" for certain records
- Confirm WORM requirement was eliminated May 2017

**Step 4: Check for missing record types**
Identify any agent-specific record types NOT in the current matrix:
- Agent model validation records — which retention period?
- Agent approval/governance records — which retention period?
- Agent incident reports — which retention period?
- Agent bias testing results — which retention period?
- AI marketing substantiation files — FINRA 4511 suggests 7 years for 2210 communications

**Step 5: Check SEC 17a-3/4 overview section accuracy**
The SEC 17a-3/4 section in regulatory-mappings.md (lines 223-283) has issues:
- Overview says "6 years" generically — should clarify varies by record type
- "Agent Communications" subsection says "Retention: 6 years" — conflicts with Retention Period Matrix (which says 3 years for communications)
- Verify and document this inconsistency
